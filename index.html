Our kitchen

Cooked the way grandmothers taught it

Nham opened in 2019 with one goal: bring the flavours of a Phnom Penh home kitchen to a restaurant table, without smoothing out the edges. Everything is made in-house daily, from the prahok to the palm sugar caramel — even the bread is baked fresh each morning.
